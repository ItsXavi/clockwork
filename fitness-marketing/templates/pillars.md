# Content pillar cheat sheet — XFITTV

Brand = **bodybuilding + gym humor + cooking** (never CrossFit/WOD)

## Bodybuilding Training (35%)
- Hypertrophy sessions, push/pull/legs
- Form cues and working-set demos
- Progressive overload tips

## Cooking & High-Protein Meals (25%)
- Fast macros that still taste good
- Meal prep that isn’t sad
- Pre/post training food

## Gym Humor (20%)
- Relatable gym moments
- Light roasts, not mean
- Always circles back to lifting culture

## Mindset & Discipline (10%)
- Show-up habits
- Missed-session minimums

## Physique & Progress (5%)
- Honest progress notes
- Form unlocks

## Community & CTA (5%)
- Keyword CTAs (RECIPE / PROTEIN / DONE)
- Challenges and tags
